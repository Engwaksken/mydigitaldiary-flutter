Android local_auth requirement

Ensure LaunchTheme inherits from an AppCompat theme.

Example:

<style name="LaunchTheme" parent="Theme.AppCompat.DayNight.NoActionBar">
    <item name="android:windowBackground">@drawable/launch_background</item>
</style>

Keep your existing launch-background items; only use an AppCompat-compatible
parent if your current theme is not already compatible.
